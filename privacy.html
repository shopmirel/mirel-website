Privacy Policy

October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mirel, a handmade brand based in Bangalore, India, collects, uses and protects the information you share when you use this website.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email from Google. If you sign up with an email and password, your password is handled by the secure sign-in service and we never see or store it in readable form.
  • Your orders: your phone number, delivery address, the items you order, and the status of each order.
  • Your shop activity: the items in your bag and your wishlist, so they are still there when you come back.
  • Notes and exchange requests: if you leave a note about a piece, we collect what you write, your star rating and any photos you add. If you request an exchange, we collect your reason and the photos you send.
  • Messages on WhatsApp: when you message us on WhatsApp, we see your WhatsApp number and the email linked to your account, so we can find your order.

How we use it

  • To make, pack and deliver your order, and to keep you updated on it.
  • To let you sign in, see your past orders, and request an exchange.
  • To show customer notes on the website, with the name you gave, and only after we have reviewed them.
  • To answer your messages and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it

Your information is stored with trusted service providers that run this website for us: secure cloud services provided by Google (accounts and database), our website hosting provider, and a private tool we use to keep track of orders. Payments are processed by a licensed payment partner, and we never see or store your card details.

We share your name, phone number and address with anyone who delivers on our behalf only when needed to deliver your order. We may also share information if the law requires us to.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember small things like your sign-in, your bag and your last used email. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order details for as long as your account exists, and order records for as long as needed for exchanges and basic record keeping. You can ask us to delete them sooner (see below).

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and orders. Message us using the email below and we will take care of it. You can also sign out at any time.

Keeping it safe

Access to orders and customer details is limited to the shop owner, and the website uses encrypted connections. No website can promise perfect security, but we take care with what you trust us with.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information? Email get.mirel@gmail.com, or use Reach Me on the shop.
