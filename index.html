Care & Size Guide

Every piece is made by hand and made to last. A little kindness goes a long way — here is how to measure for the perfect fit, and how to keep your keepsake beautiful for years.

The Size Guide
Made to your measurements

Wearables are crocheted to the four measurements you choose at checkout — each in two-inch steps from 26 to 36 inches. No S, M or L guessing.

Bustfullest part of the chest
Measure over thin clothing or on skin, tape snug but not tight.
Upper Bustjust under the arms
Under Bustribcage, right under the bust
Waistnarrowest part of the waist
How to measure: keep the tape snug but never tight, and breathe normally. Use the fullest part of the chest for the bust, just under the arms for the upper bust, the ribcage directly under the bust for the under bust, and the narrowest part of your waist for the waist. When in doubt, size up — crochet has a gentle stretch.
The Care Guide
How to keep it beautiful

Handmade yarn is kind to the earth and asks for a little kindness back.

Wash gently

Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water with a mild detergent. Avoid bleach and harsh chemicals. If you prefer the machine, use a mesh laundry bag and the gentlest cold cycle.

Never wring or twist

Squeezing and twisting pulls the stitches out of shape. Press the water out gently, or roll the piece in a clean towel to soak up the excess.

Dry flat, out of the sun

Reshape your piece while it is still damp and lay it flat on a clean, dry towel. Keep it away from direct sunlight and heaters. Give it at least 12 hours, then turn it once to finish drying.

Skip the iron

Crochet rarely needs an iron. If it must be pressed, use the lowest heat with a cloth in between — or a gentle steam from a distance. Never press directly on high.

Store folded, never hung

Hangers stretch the stitches over time. Fold your pieces and keep them in a cotton bag or an airtight tub, with a cedar or lavender sachet to keep moths away.

Tend little snags & pills

Keep jewellery away from the yarn to avoid snags. If pills appear, a gentle fabric shaver brings the surface back. A quick hand wash and re-shape restores the fit every time.
